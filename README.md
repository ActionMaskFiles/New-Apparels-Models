Custom Bootleg Apparels for the game Tales of pirates also know as King of Pirates or PKO.

Place the .lgo and .bmp files into your client's model/character and texture/character folders.

Create a new item ID in your client/server item tables (ItemInfo.txt) and assign the equipment slot
and character body restriction.

Compile your tables, sync client and server files, and test all animations in-game, hope you like it :).
